2024 Honorees

This year's cohort celebrates mentorship, innovation, and the stories behind the plates. Explore the people redefining New York hospitality.

Chef of the Year

Chef Maya Liao — Hearth & Harbor

Reimagining Cantonese cuisine through seasonal tasting menus.

Chef Liao mentors young cooks through Chinatown community programs and collaborates with local farms to highlight New York terroir in classic dishes.

Restaurateur

Jorge Alvarez — Mercado Unido

Championing immigrant-owned food stalls under one roof.

Alvarez built a multi-borough team that provides kitchen incubators, language support, and equitable profit-sharing for vendors launching their first brick-and-mortar spaces.

Food Journalist

Priya Desai — The Midnight Dispatch

Giving voice to underreported dining stories citywide.

Desai's late-night dispatches spotlight essential workers feeding New York, from halal cart legends to small bakeries baking through the night.

Community Advocate

Marcus Reed — Bed-Stuy Food Co-Op

Expanding access to fresh produce with dignity.

Reed's co-op trains teens in culinary arts and partners with local chefs for pop-up dinners that fund neighborhood food justice programs.

Pastry Visionary

Chef Alessia Romano — Dolci Notte

Bringing Italian pastry artistry to midnight dessert bars.

Romano's dessert tastings pair inventive gelati with zero-proof cocktails and showcase seasonal ingredients from regional farms.

Lifetime Achievement

Chef Emeritus Samuel Ortiz — La Isla Verde

Five decades of mentorship in Puerto Rican cuisine.

Ortiz continues to mentor chefs across the city and hosts community cookouts that preserve generational recipes and cultural history.
